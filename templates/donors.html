ELIGIBILITY

Basic Donor Requirements

Verify these physical standards before beginning your donation journey.

Age

Must be between 18 and 65 years old.

Weight

Must weigh at least 50 kg or more.

Good Health

Feeling energetic and healthy on the day of donation.

Valid ID

Bring a government photo ID or donor card.

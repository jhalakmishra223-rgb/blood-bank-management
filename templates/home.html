Why Give Blood?

A single donation takes less than an hour, but its impact lasts a lifetime for those in need.

Save Up to 3 Lives

Your single blood donation is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets, helping multiple patients.

Free Health Checkup

Before every donation, get a mini-physical checking pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels.

Boost Wellbeing

Regular donation helps reduce excess iron stores in the body and promotes a powerful sense of community contribution.

Before You Donate
  • Get adequate rest and sleep before your appointment.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking extra fluids before and after.
  • Eat a healthy, well-balanced meal prior to donating.
  • Bring a valid government-issued photo identification.
  • Follow all instructions provided by the medical personnel.

Eligibility and donation suitability are determined by qualified healthcare professionals.
